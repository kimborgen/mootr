# Retros

When something notable happens in your work, record it with the `retro` tool: a test loop that wasted time, an instruction that misled you, a trick that worked, or an idea. Search first with `retro` and `search`, and +1 a match with `plus_one` and your own scenario instead of filing a near-duplicate. Otherwise give `kind`, `title` (1-4 words), `summary` (one line), `details`, `evidence` (human, gate, review, rollback, measured, or none), `when` (the condition it applies under), and, for improve and instruction items, `target` (the block, tool, script or file). Keep each item short.
